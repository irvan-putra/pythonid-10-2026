Three demos, three study programs
=================================

Demo 0: hands-free slides
  Say "next" or "back": Whisper changes the slide

Demo 1 (Teknik Informatika): computer vision
  YOLO counts people and raised hands, live
  -> Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Pengolahan Citra Digital

Demo 2 (Sistem Informasi): live polling dashboard
  Raised hands become votes in a Streamlit dashboard
  -> Analisis Big Data, Visualisasi Data, Kecerdasan Bisnis

Demo 3 (Sistem Komputer): network protocols and interfacing
  The phone's accelerometer streams over MQTT, its camera and mic
  over WebSockets, and the results reach the dashboards over MQTT
  -> Interfacing, Sistem Tertanam, Komunikasi Data
