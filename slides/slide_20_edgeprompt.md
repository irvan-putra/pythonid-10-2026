EdgePrompt: guardrails for LLMs in K-12 education
=================================================

A prompt engineering framework for safe LLM use in schools,
using structured prompting inspired by neural-symbolic principles.

Why: educational disparities in Indonesia's 3T regions
(Terdepan, Terluar, Tertinggal), where connectivity is limited
-> content safety controls that also work offline

How, in three parts:
1. content generation with structured constraint templates
2. assessment processing with layered validation
3. lightweight storage for content and results

A multi-stage verification workflow keeps safety boundaries
while preserving what the model can do.

Proposed first deployment: Bahasa Indonesia & English class.
